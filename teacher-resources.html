What students are learning

Learning focusIn plain English
Sustainability, design and productionExplain how design and production choices affect sustainability.
Designers and producersDescribe how people investigate, plan, make and improve solutions.
Materials, systems and componentsExplain how parts, materials and systems affect how a solution works.
Project planning and productionPlan, manage and produce the project in a clear sequence.
Design communication and evaluationCommunicate ideas and judge solutions against criteria.
Safe practical workChoose and use approved tools, materials, technologies and processes safely.
Safe digital literacyWork safely and responsibly in digital environments.
Data, coding and digital systemsUse data or digital systems where they support the unit's design and production.
